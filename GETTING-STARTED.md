## Common First-Month Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Reading Everything, Applying Nothing
**Symptom:** You've read all 14 skills but haven't used any in real work.

**Fix:** Pick ONE framework. Use it this week. Deeply.

### Mistake 2: Tracking Everything
**Symptom:** You have 10 metrics, check analytics 5x/day, feel busy but directionless.

**Fix:** Delete all but 2 metrics for your stage. Hide the rest.

### Mistake 3: Skipping the Uncomfortable Work
**Symptom:** You know you should talk to customers but you're "optimizing the landing page."

**Fix:** Set SMIT to the uncomfortable thing. Do it first. Every day.

### Mistake 4: Using the System as Procrastination
**Symptom:** Spending 2 hours on weekly review, 30 minutes talking to customers.

**Fix:** System is 10 min/day, 45 min/week, 2 hours/month. Everything else is real work.

### Mistake 5: Not Being Honest
**Symptom:** Weekly review says "making progress" but metrics show otherwise.

**Fix:** Brutal honesty. Only evidence, not feelings.

If you catch yourself in 3+ of these: Stop. Reread [founder-os/SKILL.md](founder-os/SKILL.md) Honesty Rule section.

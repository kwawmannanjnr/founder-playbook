# Founder Excellence Checklist

> The 20 habits that separate the best founders from the rest. Check yourself daily.

---

## Daily Excellence (10 minutes every morning)

### 1. Know Your Stage
- [ ] I can clearly state: Problem Validation / Solution Validation / Business Model / Growth
- [ ] I know the specific success criteria to move to next stage
- [ ] I'm not jumping stages prematurely

### 2. Work the Constraint
- [ ] I can name THE ONE thing blocking progress (not 5 things)
- [ ] Today's work directly addresses that constraint
- [ ] I'm not avoiding the uncomfortable work

### 3. Clear SMIT (Single Most Important Task)
- [ ] I have ONE most important task for today
- [ ] It has specific completion criteria (not "work on X")
- [ ] It uses a specific framework/method
- [ ] It's completable in one day

### 4. Protect Focus Time
- [ ] I've blocked 4+ uninterrupted hours for SMIT
- [ ] I'm saying no to meetings that don't move the constraint
- [ ] I'm not context-switching between 10 projects

---

## Weekly Excellence (Friday review)

### 5. Measure Reality, Not Vanity
- [ ] I'm tracking only 2 metrics for my stage
- [ ] Both metrics are about customer behavior (not my activity)
- [ ] I'm using real data, not gut feel
- [ ] I'm not cherry-picking timeframes to look good

### 6. Talk to Customers Weekly
- [ ] I had 3+ real customer conversations this week
- [ ] I asked about past behavior, not future intentions (Mom Test)
- [ ] I documented what they actually said (not my interpretation)
- [ ] I'm looking for patterns, not collecting compliments

### 7. Test Assumptions, Don't Just Build
- [ ] I tested at least one hypothesis this week
- [ ] I know what result would prove it true or false
- [ ] I'm measuring learning, not just shipping
- [ ] I'm willing to pivot based on evidence

### 8. Use Frameworks, Don't Wing It
- [ ] I referenced 1-2 frameworks deeply this week
- [ ] I completed their worksheets/checklists, not just skimmed
- [ ] I applied their methods to real decisions
- [ ] I'm not just "reading about" frameworks

---

## Monthly Excellence (Strategic review)

### 9. Brutal Honesty About Progress
- [ ] I write what actually happened, not what I wished happened
- [ ] I admit when a month's goal wasn't met
- [ ] I identify the real reason (not excuses)
- [ ] I change strategy when evidence demands it

### 10. Stage Assessment
- [ ] I honestly assess if I should stay/move/pivot
- [ ] I have specific evidence for the decision
- [ ] I'm not staying in a stage out of fear
- [ ] I'm not advancing stages to feel productive

### 11. Kill Bad Patterns Early
- [ ] I check for anti-patterns (lots of interest / no money, etc)
- [ ] I catch them within 2-4 weeks, not 6 months
- [ ] I change approach when pattern is identified
- [ ] I'm not repeating the same failed tactics

### 12. Prioritize Ruthlessly
- [ ] I have 3 priorities max for next month (not 10)
- [ ] I can explain why each one matters
- [ ] I have explicit "NOT doing" list
- [ ] I'm saying no to 80% of opportunities

---

## Decision Excellence (When making significant choices)

### 13. Framework-Driven Decisions
- [ ] I identified which framework applies to this decision
- [ ] I worked through its decision tree or scorecard
- [ ] I documented the reasoning (not just the conclusion)
- [ ] I set success/fail criteria to review later

### 14. Evidence Over Opinions
- [ ] I have real data (customer conversations, metrics, experiments)
- [ ] I'm not deciding based on competitor moves
- [ ] I'm not deciding based on what "everyone says"
- [ ] I know what would change my mind

### 15. Speed vs Irreversibility
- [ ] If reversible: I decided in <30 minutes
- [ ] If irreversible: I spent 2+ hours and talked to 2+ people
- [ ] I'm not agonizing over reversible decisions
- [ ] I'm not rushing irreversible decisions

---

## Customer Excellence (Every conversation)

### 16. Mom Test Every Conversation
- [ ] I'm asking about past, not future ("last time" not "would you")
- [ ] I'm digging for specifics, not accepting vague answers
- [ ] I'm looking for commitment currencies (time, money, access)
- [ ] I'm not pitching; I'm learning

### 17. Qualify Earlyvangelists
- [ ] I'm scoring people on earlyvangelist criteria (four-steps)
- [ ] I'm focusing on people who built workarounds (have real pain)
- [ ] I'm not building for "anyone interested"
- [ ] I'm saying no to people who don't qualify

### 18. Always Talk About Price
- [ ] I'm asking about willingness-to-pay in every conversation
- [ ] I'm learning what they spend now on alternatives
- [ ] I'm designing product+price together (not price after)
- [ ] I'm not afraid to ask for money

---

## Personal Excellence (Founder health)

### 19. Sustainable Pace
- [ ] I'm sleeping 7+ hours most nights
- [ ] I'm exercising 2+ times per week
- [ ] I'm maintaining important relationships
- [ ] I'm taking at least 1 day off per week

### 20. Energy Management
- [ ] I know my energy level (1-10) right now
- [ ] I do SMIT during high-energy hours
- [ ] I batch low-energy work (admin, email)
- [ ] I'm not "powering through" burnout

## The Meta-Checklist

> How to use this list:

### Daily (2 minutes)
Check items 1-4 every morning.  
If you can't check all 4: Something is wrong. Stop and diagnose.

### Weekly (5 minutes)
Check items 5-8 during Friday review.  
Score: [X]/4 this week.  
If <2: You're drifting. Recommit to the system.

### Monthly (10 minutes)
Check all 20 items during monthly strategic review.  
Score: [X]/20 this month.

- 18-20: Excellent - keep going
- 15-17: Good - identify weak areas
- 12-14: Warning - need course correction
- <12: Crisis - stop and reset

---

## Common Patterns

### Great founders (18-20 checks):
- Work on constraints, not around them
- Talk to customers constantly
- Use frameworks systematically
- Pivot based on evidence
- Take care of themselves

### Struggling founders (12-14 checks):
- Work hard but unfocused
- Build without validating
- Know frameworks but don't apply them
- Avoid uncomfortable truths
- Burn out

### Failing founders (<12 checks):
- Guess instead of validate
- Measure vanity metrics
- Make emotion-based decisions
- Avoid customers
- Work unsustainably

---

## If Your Score Is Low

### Score 12-14 (Warning)
**Action:** Pick your 3 lowest-scoring items.  
Focus on improving those 3 this month.  
Recheck at end of month.

### Score <12 (Crisis)
**Action:** Stop everything.  
Take 2-3 days to:
1. Read `founder-os/SKILL.md` completely
2. Run diagnose skill to identify stage
3. Talk to 5 customers (no building)
4. Reset with `founder-os/quick-start.md`

## The Honest Questions

Beyond the checklist, ask yourself:

**Am I making progress?**
(Look at your 2 metrics, not your feelings)

**Am I working on what matters?**
(Is today's work moving the constraint?)

**Am I being honest with myself?**
(Read your last weekly review - were you truthful?)

**Am I still enjoying this?**
(If no for 3+ months: something needs to change)

**Would I start this today knowing what I know?**
(If no: Why are you still doing it?)

---

## The One-Sentence Test

> "A founder who _______ is doing it right."

Fill in the blank with ONE thing. If you can't pick one, you're trying to do too much.

**Examples:**
- "...talks to customers every single day"
- "...measures real metrics, not vanity"
- "...works on the constraint, not around it"
- "...pivots based on evidence, not emotion"
- "...uses frameworks systematically"

What's your one sentence? ________________

That's your North Star. Check if today's work aligns.

## The Excellence Loop

```
Every day:
  Check constraint → Set SMIT → Do it → Measure result

Every week:
  Review metrics → Check frameworks → Plan next week

Every month:
  Assess stage → Check patterns → Reset strategy

Every conversation:
  Mom Test questions → Qualify earlyvangelist → Talk price

Every decision:
  Identify framework → Work through method → Document reasoning
```

This is the loop. The best founders run this loop consistently.

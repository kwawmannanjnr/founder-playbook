# Founder OS Quick Start

> Get started in 30 minutes. No theory, just action.

---

## Day 1: Setup (30 minutes)

### Step 1: Identify Your Stage (5 minutes)

Answer this honestly:

```
Have you talked to 50+ potential customers about their problem?
|-- NO  → You're in PROBLEM VALIDATION
|-- YES → Do you have 10+ paying customers?
    |-- NO  → You're in SOLUTION VALIDATION
    |-- YES → Is CAC < LTV/3?
        |-- NO  → You're in BUSINESS MODEL VALIDATION
        |-- YES → You're in GROWTH
```

**Your stage:** ________________

---

### Step 2: Set Your Two Metrics (5 minutes)

Based on your stage, copy these:

**Problem Validation:**
- Primary: # of customer conversations (target: 50)
- Secondary: % with emotion + specifics (target: >60%)

**Solution Validation:**
- Primary: # of paying customers (target: 10)
- Secondary: 30-day retention % (target: >40%)

**Business Model Validation:**
- Primary: CAC/LTV ratio (target: <1:3)
- Secondary: Time to first value (target: <7 days)

**Growth:**
- Primary: Week-over-week growth % (target: >10%)
- Secondary: Channel contribution (target: 2-3 channels = 80%)

**Your metrics:**
- Primary: ________________
- Secondary: ________________

---

### Step 3: Identify Your Constraint (10 minutes)

Use the diagnose skill or answer these:

```
What's the ONE thing blocking you from moving to the next stage?

Common constraints:
- Can't find potential customers to talk to → DISTRIBUTION
- Don't understand what they want → CUSTOMER DISCOVERY
- They like it but won't pay → PRICING/POSITIONING
- They don't understand what I do → MESSAGING
- I have customers but can't scale → CHANNEL/PROCESS

Your constraint: ________________
```

---

### Step 4: Plan Tomorrow's Work (10 minutes)

Use the Daily Standup template (`templates/daily-standup.md`).

Fill out just the SMIT section:

```
Tomorrow's SMIT: [One task addressing your constraint]
Done when: [Specific completion criteria]
Time budget: [X hours]
```

**Examples by constraint:**

If your constraint is DISTRIBUTION:
> SMIT: "Send 15 cold DMs to [target persona] who posted about [problem] in last 7 days"

If your constraint is CUSTOMER DISCOVERY:
> SMIT: "Conduct 5 mom-test conversations, record their exact words about the problem"

If your constraint is PRICING:
> SMIT: "Run 3 WTP conversations using monetizing-innovation script"

If your constraint is MESSAGING:
> SMIT: "Rewrite homepage hero using storybrand BrandScript, test with 5 strangers"

**Your SMIT:** ________________

## Day 2-5: Build the Habit (10 min/day)

### Every Morning

1. Open `templates/daily-standup.md`
2. Copy it to `daily-standups/[DATE].md`
3. Fill it out (takes 10 minutes)
4. Do your SMIT before anything else

### Every Evening (5 minutes)

Mark SMIT as done or not done.  
Write one sentence: what blocked it or what result it produced.  
Draft tomorrow's SMIT.

---

## End of Week 1: First Review (45 minutes)

Friday afternoon, use `templates/weekly-review.md`:

### Fill Out The Numbers (15 minutes)
- How many SMITs completed? (Target: 3-5)
- What were your two metrics at start of week vs now?
- Best customer insight this week?

### Framework Check (15 minutes)
- Which framework did you use most?
- Rate yourself 0-10 on that framework
- If <7: What will you do differently?

### Next Week Plan (15 minutes)
- Same constraint or new one?
- Three most important tasks for next week
- What are you explicitly NOT doing?

---

## Week 2-4: Optimization

### Keep Doing
- Daily standup (10 min/morning)
- Weekly review (45 min/Friday)
- Focus on moving one constraint

### Add When Ready
- Track your two metrics in `templates/metrics-dashboard.md`

## Month 2: Strategic Review

First Friday of month 2, use `templates/monthly-strategic-review.md`:

This is your most important review. Block 2 hours, no distractions.

You'll assess:
- Are you in the right stage?
- Should you pivot?
- Which frameworks are helping vs hurting?
- What's your plan for next month?

## The One Rule

**Work on the constraint, not around it.**

If your constraint is "don't know if people want this" (PROBLEM VALIDATION):
- ✅ Talking to potential customers
- ❌ Building features, designing website, setting up analytics

If your constraint is "people won't pay" (SOLUTION VALIDATION):
- ✅ Testing pricing, running WTP conversations, asking for money
- ❌ Adding features, optimizing performance, perfecting copy

If your constraint is "can't find customers" (DISTRIBUTION):
- ✅ Testing channels, doing outreach, running ads
- ❌ Improving product, refactoring code, analyzing competitors

If your constraint is "confused messaging" (MESSAGING):
- ✅ Rewriting copy, running 5-second tests, A/B testing hero section
- ❌ Building features, customer support, internal process

**Everything else is avoidance.**

## When You Get Stuck

### "I don't know what constraint to work on"

Run the diagnose skill (`diagnose/SKILL.md`).  
It will route you to the right framework.

### "I'm working on it but nothing's improving"

Check Anti-Pattern Detector in `founder-os/SKILL.md`.  
You're probably:
1. In the wrong stage
2. Measuring the wrong thing
3. Using the wrong framework

### "I feel overwhelmed"

You're trying to do too much.

**The fix:**
1. Pick ONE constraint
2. Pick ONE framework
3. Pick ONE metric
4. Work on it for 1 week
5. Review on Friday

Repeat.

### "This system feels like overhead"

Then you're using it wrong.

The system should:
- Take 10 min/day (daily standup)
- Take 45 min/week (weekly review)
- Make you MORE focused, not less

If it's taking longer or making you less focused:
- You're tracking too many things (cut to 2 metrics)
- You're using too many frameworks (pick 1-2 per month)
- You're not being specific enough (SMITs should be concrete)

## Common Mistakes in First Month

### Mistake 1: Jumping Between Frameworks

**Symptom:** You use mom-test on Monday, lean-startup on Tuesday, traction on Wednesday.

**Fix:** Pick ONE framework per week. Go deep, not broad.

### Mistake 2: Tracking Everything

**Symptom:** You're tracking 10 metrics, checking analytics 5x/day, feel busy but directionless.

**Fix:** Pick 2 metrics for your stage. Hide the rest.

### Mistake 3: Vague SMITs

**Symptom:** Your SMIT is "work on marketing" or "improve product."

**Fix:** Make it specific: "Send 20 DMs to iOS devs" or "Add email capture to homepage."

### Mistake 4: Avoiding the Uncomfortable Work

**Symptom:** You know you should talk to customers but you're "optimizing the landing page" instead.

**Fix:** Do the uncomfortable thing first. Every day. Non-negotiable.

### Mistake 5: Not Using the Frameworks

**Symptom:** You're doing daily standups but not opening any framework skill files.

**Fix:** Each SMIT should reference a framework. "Using mom-test questions" or "Running traction Bullseye test."

## The Meta-Point

This system exists to make you a better founder, not a better system-follower.

**Good signs:**
- You're making faster progress
- You're more focused
- You catch mistakes earlier
- You make better decisions

**Bad signs:**
- You're "doing the system" but not moving metrics
- You're filling out templates instead of talking to customers
- You feel busy but directionless
- The system is taking more time than it saves

If you see bad signs: Stop. Read the "Honest Rule" section in founder-os/SKILL.md.

The system works when you use it honestly. It fails when you game it.

## Next Steps

1. **Today:** Identify stage, set metrics, write tomorrow's SMIT
2. **Tomorrow:** Do your first daily standup
3. **Friday:** Do your first weekly review
4. **Next Month:** Do your first monthly strategic review

Then repeat. Month after month. Until you either:
- Succeed (metrics consistently improving, reached product-market fit)
- Pivot intelligently (learned what doesn't work, testing new hypothesis)
- Kill it cleanly (learned valuable lessons, moving to next thing)

All three are winning. The only losing move is working hard on the wrong thing for too long.

This system helps you avoid that.

## Questions?

**"Which framework should I start with?"**  
Run diagnose skill. It will tell you.

**"Can I skip daily standups?"**  
You can, but then don't blame the system when you're unfocused.

**"Do I need all the templates?"**  
No. Start with daily-standup.md and weekly-review.md. Add others as needed.

**"What if my situation doesn't fit any framework?"**  
Then stop using frameworks. Go talk to customers. Come back when you have clarity.

**"How do I know if this is working?"**  
Your two metrics should improve week-over-week. If not after 4 weeks, something's wrong.

**"Can I customize this?"**  
Yes. But use it as-written for 1 month first. Then adapt based on what actually helps you.

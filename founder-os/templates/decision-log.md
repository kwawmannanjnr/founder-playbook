# Decision Log

> Record every significant decision. Your future self will want to know what you were thinking.

---

## How to Use This

1. Before making any significant decision, fill out a decision entry
2. Write what you decided AND why
3. Record what evidence you had (or didn't have)
4. Set a review date to check if you were right

**What counts as "significant":**
- Changes strategy (pivot, new market, new product direction)
- Commits resources (hiring, major spend, partnership)
- Is hard to reverse (legal, contracts, public commitments)

**What doesn't need logging:**
- Tactical execution decisions (which tool to use, minor copy changes)
- Easily reversible experiments (which email subject line, small A/B tests)

## Decision Examples

### Example 1: Pivot Decision

**Decision #1 - 2026-04-15**

**The Decision**  
Pivot from horizontal "content scheduler for everyone" to vertical "LinkedIn content engine for B2B SaaS founders"

**Context**  
After 3 months, we have 200 signups but only 2 paying users. High signup-to-trial ratio but terrible trial-to-paid. Users aren't seeing enough value. The 2 paying users are both B2B SaaS founders using it specifically for LinkedIn.

**Options Considered**

A: Keep current positioning, improve onboarding
B: Pivot to vertical (B2B SaaS founders + LinkedIn)
C: Pivot to different horizontal (better competitor differentiation)
D: Kill the product

**What We Decided**  
Option B - Pivot to vertical

**Why We Decided This**

Primary reason: The 2 paying customers are both in the same segment (B2B SaaS founders) using same use case (LinkedIn). Pattern in 2 is worth investigating.

Key evidence:
- Obviously Awesome positioning: we were Head-to-Head in "content scheduling" (crowded, commoditized)
- 15 customer conversations showed founders have specific pain: LinkedIn is their #1 lead gen channel but they're inconsistent
- They're already paying for LinkedIn tools ($50-200/mo) - proven willingness to pay
- Lean Startup: Customer Segment Pivot - same solution, different customer

Framework applied: Obviously Awesome 5+1 (competitive alternatives → positioning) + Lean Startup pivot catalog

What we could be wrong about: Sample size of 2 is tiny. Founders might be willing to pay $30/mo but not $100/mo.

**Reversibility:** Moderately reversible - can rebuild horizontal positioning in 2-3 weeks if this fails

**Success Criteria**

We'll know we were right if:
- 5+ B2B SaaS founders convert to paid within 30 days
- Trial-to-paid conversion >15% (vs current 1%)
- NPS >40 from this segment

We'll know we were wrong if:
- <3 conversions in 30 days
- Founders say "nice but I'll use free tier"
- Churn rate >10%/month

**Review date:** 2026-05-15

## Red Flag Checklist

Before finalizing ANY significant decision, check:

- [ ] Did I use a framework or just wing it?
- [ ] Do I have evidence or just opinions?
- [ ] Did I talk to customers about this?
- [ ] Am I deciding based on competitors' actions?
- [ ] Am I making this decision to avoid a harder decision?
- [ ] Have I steel-manned the opposite choice?
- [ ] Do I have clear success/fail criteria?
- [ ] If this is irreversible, did I spend enough time?

**If you checked 3+ boxes:** Don't decide yet. Investigate more.

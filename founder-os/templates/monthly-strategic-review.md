## Red Flag Review

Check if any of these patterns appeared this month:

**Strategic Red Flags:**
- [ ] Pivoted or changed strategy mid-month without clear reason
- [ ] Chased a big opportunity that wasn't in the plan
- [ ] Got distracted by competitor moves
- [ ] Changed target customer segment more than once
- [ ] Added "just one more feature" before launching

**Execution Red Flags:**
- [ ] Didn't complete 60%+ of SMITs for the month
- [ ] Spent >30% of time firefighting  
- [ ] Had fewer than 10 customer conversations
- [ ] Didn't test any new distribution channels
- [ ] Avoided the uncomfortable task I identified

**Metrics Red Flags:**
- [ ] Primary metric flat or declining for 2+ months
- [ ] Changed which metric I'm tracking to show progress
- [ ] Measuring signups/traffic instead of revenue/retention
- [ ] Don't know my CAC or LTV
- [ ] Celebrating metrics that don't move business forward

**Founder Health Red Flags:**
- [ ] Worked 7 days/week multiple weeks in a row
- [ ] Sleep-deprived more days than not
- [ ] Skipped exercise/health routines for 2+ weeks
- [ ] Relationships suffering from work obsession
- [ ] Feeling burned out or apathetic

**If 3+ red flags checked:** Something is structurally wrong. Consider:
1. Taking a week to reset and reflect
2. Finding a co-founder or advisor for support
3. Simplifying scope (you may be trying to do too much)
4. Evaluating if this is the right opportunity
